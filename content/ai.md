# Creative Work
I will never use generative AI to make art, images, videos, music, or prose. I will make a best effort to ensure that no art, images, videos, music or prose that I cite or include in any of my published online content were made in whole or in part with generative AI. I will remove any art, images, videos, music, or prose that I am informed were made in whole or in part with generative AI.

# Code
I may use the outputs of free-tier generative AI as reference to learn or to troubleshoot unfamiliar technology, and may copy short (less than 10 lines) snippets of boilerplate code. Any uses of generative AI that are more substantial than that will be rare, prominently disclosed, noncommercial, and explicitly justified. As of 2026-10-09, I have yet to publish any work that was created with generative AI beyond using Google AI overview as a reference.
